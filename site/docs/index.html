Summaries of scientific papers, each linked to the publisher's page.

  • Measurements of the Heat and Mass Transfer Parameters Characterizing Conical Graupel GrowthCober, S. G.; List, R. · 1993 · Journal of the Atmospheric Sciences, 50(11), 1591-1609

    Conical graupel grown on fixed stems in an icing wind tunnel, with surface temperature measured by infrared imaging, yield parameterizations of collection efficiency, Nusselt number, rime density and cone angle; collection efficiency is about 25% below smooth-sphere theory and heat transfer about 50% above smooth cones.

  • A Method to Determine Precipitation TypesBourgouin, P. · 2000 · Weather and Forecasting, 15, 583–592

    The paper introduces the "area method", which diagnoses surface freezing rain, ice pellets, rain and snow from the areas between a temperature profile and the 0°C isotherm on a tephigram, and shows it outperforms the methods then used in Canada.

  • A Statistical and Physical Description of Hydrometeor Distributions in Colorado Snowstorms Using a Video DisdrometerBrandes, E. A.; Ikeda, K.; Zhang, G.; Schönhuber, M.; Rasmussen, R. M. · 2007 · Journal of Applied Meteorology and Climatology, 46, 634–650

    Using ground-based two-dimensional video disdrometer (2DVD) data from Colorado snowstorms, the paper fits exponential and gamma size distributions, derives a snow density–size relation ρs = 0.178 D0^−0.922, and gives temperature, snowfall-rate and shape–slope (μ–Λ) relations for models and radar.

  • Evaluating the Performance of Planetary Boundary Layer and Cloud Microphysical Parameterization Schemes in Convection-Permitting Ensemble Forecasts Using Synthetic GOES-13 Satellite ObservationsCintineo, R.; Otkin, J. A.; Xue, M.; Kong, F. · 2014 · Monthly Weather Review, 142, 163-182

    Comparing synthetic GOES-13 infrared images from 4-km WRF ensemble forecasts with observations over 20 days, the paper finds that Milbrandt–Yau and Morrison produce too much upper-level cloud, WDM6 too little and Thompson the closest to observed, and that the PBL scheme matters much less than the microphysics.
